I'm an Assistant Professor in econometrics and data science at the Department of Economics at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU).
I'm also a Visiting Scholar at the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia and an Associate Researcher at the University of Cambridge.
I did my PhD in economics at SDU, during which I spent the academic fall 2022 at Stanford University.
I'm part of the Econometrics and Data Science research platform and affiliated with the Historical Economics and Development Group (HEDG).

## My research
My research focuses on causal inference and data science, and in particular on using machine learning to study the importance of early-life conditions and to evaluate policies.
I use machine learning to create data from historical documents, and I develop methods for causal inference, including treatment effect heterogeneity in difference-in-differences designs and the optimal allocation of treatments under constraints.
I use these new data sources and methods to study the impact of health policies.
